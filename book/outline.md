# THE GILDED DOOR — working bible and chapter plan (private craft document)

## Premise and register
- Historical epic, Rome AD 112–113 (Trajan, Parthian campaign looming; Forum of Trajan newly dedicated).
- Register: Ken Follett — tight third person, rotating POV, plain polished prose, concrete sensory detail, strong plot engine, cliffhanger chapter ends, moral seriousness without cynicism, love story across class, villains who believe their own reasons.
- Tense: past. Each scene opens on a named viewpoint; no head-hopping inside a scene.
- Mature/18+: sex written as character, frank but not clinical; violence with consequence; slavery shown as the violent institution it is. No eroticized sexual violence.
- Banned slop vocabulary per repo ANTI-SLOP.md (delve, tapestry, myriad, plethora, leverage, realm, nuanced, resonate, etc.). No filler openers ("It was worth noting"). Known-new sentence contract; dialogue does plot work.
- Latin terms used sparingly, always self-explaining through context; dates and offices loose but defensible.

## Cast
- **Antonia Cassia**, 34. Born a household slave in the Aelius domus; freed and trained by the courtesan Cassia Prima, whose house she inherited and enlarged. Runs Rome's most exclusive supper-house. Keeps ledgers and secrets. Hard, protective, unsentimental.
- **Marcus Aelius Severus**, 38. Patrician senator, consul-designate for AD 113. Austere, honest, stubborn. Shared youth and love with Antonia; believes she was freed and chose to leave him.
- **Livia Marcella**, 33. Marcus's wife, ancient Julii-ish line, child-arranged marriage; proud, shrewd, trapped; her own arc toward independence.
- **Gaius Aelius**, 14, Marcus's son, bookish, principled. **Vibia**, 11, his daughter.
- **Decimus Aelius Celer**, Marcus's younger brother, charming, spendthrift, deep in debt, entangled in supply-contract fraud.
- **Chloe**, 28, Greek, the house's leading courtesan and Antonia's closest friend; sister of the murdered clerk Hector; literate, brave, dry-witted.
- **Sabina**, 17, freeborn girl surrendered for her father's debt; Antonia sees her younger self in her.
- **Festus (Hephaestio)**, 40s, ex-retiarius gladiator, freed; doorman and muscle, gentle off the sand.
- **Phyllis**, 60s, freedwoman housekeeper; once a slave in the Aelius household; the last living witness to the old betrayal; Antonia's surrogate mother.
- **Lucius Porcius Hilarus**, freedman grain-and-oil importer, Antonia's business partner; new money, buying his son toward the equestrian order.
- **Sextus Perperna**, villain. Equestrian delator (professional accuser), smooth, poor-born and ravenous; prosecutes under maiestas/lex Iulia to seize property; wants Antonia's house, its secrets, and Marcus's ruin.
- **Beryllus**, Alexandrian banker; holds everyone's paper.
- **Hector**, Greek freedman clerk on the supply board; murdered early; leaves duplicate wax tablets with Chloe.
- **Tyche**, courtesan in the house, frightened into signing false evidence; redeemed later.
- **Vibius Longus**, centurion of the vigiles; honest, tired, fire-fighter.
- **Quintus Avianius**, Sabina's father, indebted smallholder.
- **Syrus**, seal-engraver; material witness. **Macer**, pliable aedile.
- Background historicals: Trajan, Plotina, Hadrian, Prefect Acilius Attianus — kept at distance, used for texture and pressure, never anachronistic cameos.

## The buried past (released in fragments)
Antonia and Marcus grew up together in the Aelius household; he taught her letters; adolescent love; she conceived at 17. Marcus's father and mother discovered it. While Marcus was sent to a military tribunate in Germania, the mother and steward Dromo sold Antonia to a dealer; the child was stillborn in a lupanar. Marcus's letters and the money he sent for her manumission were intercepted; he was told she had been freed and refused to see him. Both have lived on opposite false versions. Phyllis carries the true one.

## Plot engine
Celer and Perperna defraud the treasury on army supply contracts ahead of the Parthian war (short-weight grain, forged seals, inflated tallies). Hector's duplicate tablets are the evidence. Perperna wants Antonia's house as an intelligence asset and its property; he uses licensing threats, a lenocinium prosecution, Sabina's contested debt, and intimidation. Antonia and Marcus are forced into alliance; the investigation and the old love reawaken together. Escalation: murder, fire, public trial. Ending costs Marcus the consulship and the possibility of legal marriage (a senatorial man cannot wed a meretrix); he chooses private station; Antonia rebuilds on her own terms; they bond as equals outside the law's categories.

## 25-chapter plan (2–3 scenes each; target 5,000–6,000 words/chapter)

### PART I — THE HOUSE (1–6)
1. Dawn after a banquet; the household's rules; Sabina delivered against her will as debt payment. Marcus's salutatio; contract review assigned; an invitation to a famous house whose mistress's name means nothing to him. Antonia alone locks away a memento.
2. Marcus attends the supper at Antonia's; the fifteen-year recognition; icy public exchange; Perperna present, watching.
3. Antonia in the Subura and at Beryllus's bank; first legal pressure via the aedile's clerk; Marcus revisits the old family house and the gap in his memory.
4. Hector secretly visits Chloe and leaves the tablets; loose senatorial pillow-talk at a banquet; Hector's body pulled from the Tiber.
5. Senate contract debate; Perperna's accusation in open session; the Aelius family dinner; Livia's discontent; Gaius's questions.
6. Perperna calls on Antonia in person with his bargain; refusal; the women study the tablets; Sabina begins lessons; first fragment of the buried past.

### PART II — DEBTS (7–12)
7. Circus Maximus races; forced proximity in the box; a crush in the crowd; Celer and Perperna exchange papers in plain sight.
8. Sabina's father begs at the door; Antonia's bargain; Sabina chooses to stay and learn; Gaius sees street Rome and meets Festus.
9. Baths of Trajan: men's politics and Hilarus's information; Livia and Antonia meet at the women's baths.
10. Hilarus's villa dinner; Antonia admitted as a business equal; public snub and Marcus's defense; first honest words alone; interrupted near-kiss.
11. Celer's debts crash into the family; Chloe deciphers the tablets; a name and a seal emerge.
12. Aedile raid on the house on a morals charge; licenses hold but fear lands; Antonia sends Marcus a message; he comes to her door at night.

### PART III — WHISPERS (13–19)
13. Nocturnal alliance; the tablets shown; terms set; first cracks in the false story of the past.
14. Colosseum games; a spy caught in the house; Livia sees the two of them across the arena.
15. Livia visits Antonia veiled; negotiation between wives; Livia begins her own inquiries.
16. Ostia: harbour accounts, short-weight grain, a beaten witness; night in a tavern; the love scene.
17. Phyllis tells the true buried past; Marcus finds Dromo's old records at the country villa; guilt and reconciliation's hardest step.
18. Perperna files charges: lenocinium, adultery, subpoenas; Tyche breaks; Marcus is told to denounce her or lose the consulship.
19. A noble wedding where all Rome watches; Livia's public move; Celer's flight stopped; the seal-maker nearly killed; Festus wounded; Antonia taken into custody.

### PART IV — PAYMENT (20–25)
20. Custody; the household rallies; Beryllus agrees to testify; declarations through a barred door.
21. The night fire set to destroy the tablets; Phyllis's death saving Sabina and the evidence; funeral rites.
22. Trial, first day in the Forum; prosecution theatre; Livia arrives with the family debt papers.
23. Trial, second day; Chloe's testimony; the forgeries exposed; Celer turns; Perperna incriminates himself; verdict and exile.
24. The price: the consulship bargain; Marcus chooses private station; divorce by mutual respect; Antonia refuses to be anyone's kept woman; the quarrel that tests everything.
25. Epilogue, AD 113: Trajan's army marching east; the rebuilt house and its new gilded door; every thread closed; the two of them at the threshold on equal terms.

## Style checklist before each chapter
- Opens with person + concrete action, never weather-throat-clearing.
- Every scene ends on a turn, question, or changed situation.
- Sensory anchors per scene: smell/sound/touch at least once.
- Roman texture earned (objects, rituals, money, streets), never lecture-dumped.
- Dialogue distinguishes voices: Antonia clipped and dry; Marcus formal then loosening; Chloe ironic; Celer charm-flustered; Perperna soft-voiced menace.
- End-of-chapter hook mandatory.
