# THE DIVIDED HARVEST — Story Bible

**Setting.** Warsaw, October–December 1958, with extended passages in the village of Brzozówka (Krasnystaw district, Lublin voivodeship), 1936–1949.

**Premise.** Zofia Krawiec, 31, deputy director of the Office for Religious Affairs, must write the state's opinion on the Church's nomination of Father Tomasz Wierzbicki, 34, as auxiliary bishop of Lublin. They were childhood sweethearts in Brzozówka. She believes she destroyed him twice: in 1944, under interrogation, she named the cellar where a wounded Soviet scout and a Jewish family were hidden, and the punitive expedition that followed burned the village and killed her mother and brother; in 1949 she named him to a Party verification commission, and her sentence entered the file that helped send him to Mokotów prison in 1951.

**What the Security Service wants.** Colonel Bolesław Wrona, Department IV (Church), wants Tomasz approved — but only if he signs a cooperation agreement. He believes Zofia is the only person who can obtain the signature. "He will do it for you."

**What Tomasz knows.** Since 1951, when interrogators showed him the translated German deposition, he has known Zofia named the cellar. He has never told anyone. In 1951 he also signed a statement naming two seminary colleagues, one of whom was expelled and one imprisoned. His letter to her, intercepted and never sent, sits in the file.

**What Zofia chooses.** She recommends approval without conditions, discloses her own record, and refuses to ask him. Consequence: removal from the Office. He is consecrated 8 December 1958 in Lublin Cathedral.

**Zofia Krawiec** — b. 3 May 1927, Brzozówka. Daughter of Józef, the village blacksmith, and Aniela. ZMP instructor in Lublin 1945–49; Party school 1950; Ministry of Culture 1952–54; Office for Religious Affairs 1954–. Married since 1953 to Jerzy Madej, Planning Commission. Lives in the MDM, ul. Piękna. Wears grey, smokes Extra, keeps a tin of buttons containing a ring made from a bicycle spoke.

**Father Tomasz Wierzbicki** — b. 6 January 1924. Son of Franciszek, village organist and carpenter. AK courier from 1942, sworn in the forest at 18. Arrested by the NKVD 9 August 1944, held at Ostashkov, released spring 1947. Seminary in Lublin 1948–53; arrested 3 October 1951, released 2 July 1952; ordained 28 June 1953; lecturer in moral theology at KUL from 1955. Author of *Głos i rana* (1957), on conscience. Thin, precise, a smoker of cheap cigarettes, plays Bach badly and honestly.

**Supporting cast.**
- *Mieczysław Brzozowski*, 63, director of the Office. Pre-war lawyer, ex-PPS. Believes the war on the Church is being fought by amateurs. Dying of stomach cancer and knows it.
- *Colonel Bolesław Wrona*, 46, SB Department IV. Polite, patient, obese, a collector of other people's signatures.
- *Hanna Ostrowska*, 24, Zofia's secretary. Types anti-religious circulars with a scapular under her blouse.
- *Jerzy Madej*, 43, Zofia's husband. Planning Commission. Decent, tired, has known about Tomasz since 1953.
- *Bishop Adam Krynicki*, 71, ordinary of Lublin. Ill, stubborn, wants Tomasz.
- *Monsignor Stefan Lubieński*, vicar general. Distrusts the candidate's youth and his AK past.
- *Father Marian Pacewicz*, 52, the alternative candidate. A "patriot priest."
- *Wiktor Sroka*, d. 1953, AL commander, Zofia's mentor; shot as a deviationist. She testified at his hearing.
- *Józef Krawiec*, d. 1953, her father. Never spoke to her again after 1952.
- *Aniela Krawiec* and *Piotrek Krawiec* (b. 1936), killed 14 June 1944.
- *Icek and Rywka Frydman* and *Sara* (b. 1934), hidden by Józef Krawiec; killed 14 June 1944.

**Timeline.** Sept 1939 invasion · 1940 secret teaching begins · 1942 Tomasz sworn into AK · Oct 1942 Krasnystaw ghetto liquidated · Nov 1942–43 Aktion Zamość · spring 1943 first kiss, wire ring · 14 June 1944 fire · 22 July 1944 PKWN manifesto; Zofia leaves with Sroka · 9 Aug 1944 NKVD arrests Tomasz · 1947 returns · 1949 her verification commission · 1951 his arrest · 1953 Sroka shot, her father dies, she marries · 1956 October thaw, Wyszyński released · 1957 *Po Prostu* closed · Oct 1958 the terna reaches the Office · 8 Dec 1958 consecration.

**Motifs.** The Wieprz river and its bend, where they meant to build a house. A ring of galvanised wire. The smell of coal smoke and lilac. The Palace of Culture against the Royal Castle still in rubble. "Serdeczna Matko" sung badly by a village choir. Bach's chorale prelude *Ich ruf zu dir, Herr Jesu Christ*. Signatures.

**Rules of the book.** Close third person on Zofia throughout; Tomasz's voice only through documents. No flashback longer than a chapter. No consolation the characters have not paid for.
