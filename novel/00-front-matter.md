# THE DIVIDED HARVEST

### A Novella in Ten Chapters — Warsaw, 1958

*Two childhood sweethearts from the village of Brzozówka, torn apart by war, meet again on opposite sides of a divided Poland: she a high-ranking cadre of the Party, he a priest nominated — at thirty-four, the youngest in modern Polish history — to a bishopric.*

*Historical note: This is a work of fiction. The Poland of 1958 — Gomułka's "Polish road to socialism," Cardinal Wyszyński two years out of internment, Warsaw rising from its ruins under the shadow of the Palace of Culture — is rendered as faithfully as the story allows. The people are invented; the world they move through was real.*
