The marriage, as marriages of settlement go, was competently unhappy only in its first season and workable thereafter, which among their kind is the material other classes call a success story. Servilia brought land in two provinces, a mind that ran the Palatine house the way Antonia's runs the Caelian — by sections, lit from the back — and the knowledge she dropped into the conversation about oysters at exactly the depth a well requires to be sounded. Lentulus brought the name, the masks, the Moesia shoulder, and a locked room whose existence he announced, as men do, by walking around it for nineteen years. They became, in time, the thing neither of their families had itemised in the contracts: allies. Alliance is not the word the poets dine out on. It outlasts most of the words they do.

There were children in the order the contracts hoped for, which Rome calls Fortune and households call management. Marcus first, loud as a gate. A daughter between who lived nine days and is spoken of in this family, if at all, in the grammar people use for weather in previous consulships — Lentulus, holding the nine-day girl on the last morning with a competence that astonished the women, whose professional opinion of senators' hands was formed elsewhere, understood for the first time the true exchange rate of the sentence his father had sold him at twenty-four: *nothing living with a claim on this house*. Nothing living was doing heavy work in that sentence, like a slave carrying water in a pleasure house. He paid the midwife — a Roman one, unhennaed, expensive — three times her fee and told the steward to record it as household linen. Servilia, coming off the childbed into a house performing a grief it was not certain it had budgeted for, looked at the accounts a month later and said nothing about the linen. It is one of perhaps six sentences of hers he has carried unspent. It had no words in it. That was the denomination.

Cornelia came three years later and stayed, with the air of a person who has reviewed the house and decided to improve it from inside, at the pace of music. By then Lentulus was on the ladder his name had built: quaestor's accounts audited like sieges, a praetorship, the consulship (suffect, the kind history pronounces with a footnote, which his father received with a toast to *the family's momentum*, as if they were a stone going downhill in good order). He governed nothing larger than his opinions in those years and governed them in public with distinction. The locked room furnished his speeches the way cellars furnish dinners: nothing was served from it directly; everything tasted of storage. When the Senate debated the exposure of infants on the hillsides — a practice every man in the chamber deplored in the manner of men deploring rain from inside good roofs — Lentulus heard his own voice arrive before his judgment, hot as a boy's, and watched the benches register *a Cornelius with a conscience*, a phrase that would follow him to the committee, to the baths, to a Greek banker's back room. When the freedwomen's tax was last revised he spoke for putting the collectors under the aediles instead of farming them out to private appetites, and carried it, and came home and drank one cup more than his rule and stood among the masks telling himself — the house has the record of it in the wear on one particular floor tile — that a man may settle a debt in general legislation if the currency is chosen well enough. His father would have agreed. That was the part that kept him standing there.

He saw Antonia twice in those years, before the Caelian house made seeing her a municipal question. Once in Naples, on business of an estate inspection: a woman at a banker's table in a widow's office, no longer carrying anything, signing with the hand he had — the locked room again; the laugh; the stylus — taught its letters, now filing a widow's loans with a competence that made the banker sit like a client. He was conducted past her room on other business. Neither of them was in a position to make it an event, and the city, which appreciates economy, recorded none: a senator looking at paperwork, a freedwoman being paperwork, a corridor functioning. The second time was Rome, the Subura, years later, rain: a dining room's doorway, lamplight, a woman sending a carter back into the street with an amphora in each hand and a sentence that had the whole street laughing with her and at nobody. He sat in his chair unrecognised behind wet leather and did not get out. He has since decided — committees are how his family thinks — that there was no decision to make, both times. A man's life arrives in the order of the evening, not the order of the questions. He did not, until the tablet, permit himself to itemise whose evening it had been.
