His reports become a feature of the Caelian mornings, filed between the carter's weather and Diomedes' first book. “Fuller was sitting by the frames.” “Fuller asked after the price of wool, mistress, and called the guild physician a Greek theatre.” “Fuller's not beaten, but the house is heating honey at hours that suggest the night staff ain't sleeping.” Barbatus delivers them standing, out of a habit older than his door, and Antonia receives them standing too, whatever room it is, a discipline her household begins, without being told, to imitate at the edges: Eirene stops arranging; Diomedes stops writing. Eleven years of staff know when information has a face.

The things that do not constitute interference are arranged with a care that would shame a foreign ministry. Diomedes, instructed to proceed as if buying thread, discovers through the guild chest that Vibius's dues purchase a physician's second visit *where decline is chronic and the householder of good standing*, a clause sleeping in the guild's papers since a consulship nobody at the vats can name. It is woken up. It is minuted. No name of the waker appears. Honey arrives at a fuller's house in winter from a supplier whose cousin works Antonia's kitchens; this is Rome; cousins are a currency. A warmer for the sickroom is conveyed by the guild's common stock because the common stock discovers, in the frost, a surplus. Antonia governs the whole campaign the way she governs a banquet for Alexandrians: from the seating. Nothing is placed where its donor would have to be looked at.

“You could simply give them money,” Eirene says, once, trimming the lamps down in the small hours when the house's Saturnalia has done its worst and best. The great room has hosted the festival in its professional form: masters pretending to serve, slaves permitted to be witty within quotations, everybody paying Antonia for an evening of inversion staged so competently that no hierarchy was actually harmed in the performance. A senator's freedman won at dice. A senator lost with the grace of a man whose grandfather is on a mask across the city saying the same. Daphne sang, for the set that is hers now by custom and contract, the slow street song at full speed, as a holiday, and made the room laugh in all the right places like a fortune returning.

“I could give them money. I could walk across the bridge with it in a bag marked *Caelian*. The neighbourhood would have the story by the watch-change: the mistress buys the fuller's daughter for a mushroom. By January it's a scandal. By the hanging's border it's her reputation unhemmed and his pride the poorer. No.” Antonia watches a servant — allowed at Saturnalia to answer back, doing it with the restraint of a man who has read his contract's new clause in his own hand at New Year and is rich now in a way the festival cannot lend him — refuse a guest's third undiluted cup with a bow. “Kindness has to travel like their other news, Eirene: through the guild, through cousins, through the plumbing. If it arrives with my name on it, it arrives as a claim. I know about claims. I have a committee's worth of education in claims, and I paid fees.”

Eirene trims. “And the clause you wrote somewhere yourself — between the quarters — the one about debts in deathwatches?”

“That one is family law. We don't minute it.” But she is looking, as she says it, at the box in Diomedes' office, where, between tax receipts, are now filed: an unsigned note that taught her a phrase; a posted text with a law in it; a December bill marked settled in copy; and nothing at all, in any hand, from across the river, because the fourth set of books is being kept where it has always been kept, and some mornings at thirty-eight, after a Saturnalia at full price, holding it feels like carrying a full glass very slowly across a courtyard she owns, among roses, in the sight of gods whose inspectors make their rounds by appointment. The word arrives. She sets it down. It does not spill. That is both the achievement and its whole cost, and Diomedes, entering with the day-book, tactfully prices something else.
