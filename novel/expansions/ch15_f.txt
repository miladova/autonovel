What she does instead — it will be very important to her later that there was an instead, and that it was work — is register. On the first morning of the new system, by appointment, at a stated hour, when a woman may be dressed for an audience with the state, Antonia Cassia attends the aediles' office in person, with Diomedes two paces behind and the first book under his arm in its upright dullness, and files her house for the quarter under the general registry that a Cornelius piloted through the Senate at the price of Syria.

The official is the one who found her form too small for the facts. He has a bigger form now. It has been designed by men who expect larger facts and have left, near the bottom, a space that a clerk in a previous reign would have used for omens. He looks at Antonia across a desk that has prosecuted market frauds since before either of them was fluent, and he does not ask about the inspection, the committee, the death between courses, or the glass that turns out, in his office's gossip, to have been emeralds all along. He asks for the names.

Diomedes gives them in the order of the evening: status, registration, manumission where manumission applies, each woman a file with a face she has consented to be filed by. The new provision is cited at the foot, in Antonia's own instruction, as the house's governing text for its contracts. The official reads it standing — officials in this winter are doing a great deal of reading standing in her presence; she files the phenomenon under draughts — and says, “The senator's clause.”

“The Senate's clause,” Antonia says. “I am told committees think in public now. Let us not spoil their education with attributions.”

He stamps the quarter. The stamp has a sound like a small, well-built door closing. Outside, Rome is registering itself at a walking pace: lodging houses, dining clubs, twenty-three categories of establishment arriving by appointment with their paperwork washed, while a scribe two stalls down is already doing a trade in copies of the posted text, whole and in extracts, the extracts principally clause-shaped. Antonia buys one. Not for her files — she has the text in Diomedes' second hand, in the box, between quarters. She buys it because on the walk home it is raining in the Roman winter way, insinuating itself into wool and acoustics, and a woman with a copy of the law in her document case walks differently through the city that wrote it at her: not safer, exactly; *itemised*. She sends the copy, that afternoon, by a carter with a history of darkness now fully reformed under house employment, to an address in Transtiberim. Not to a fuller’s house. To the fullers' guild office over the wine shop, where a clerk with a cough like a small dog will file it in the chest, among funeral lists and arrivals, with no covering letter and no name, like linen around glass.

She tells Prima nothing that day either. But a paper has gone into the girl's world that will be there when the telling is, and Antonia, who has lived by paper and been sold by it and freed by a widow's paragraph of it, knows what such advance parties are. The law goes first, quietly, and builds the road. Then, at an hour nobody's steward schedules, the people walk down it with their baskets, calling it as though it had always been there.
