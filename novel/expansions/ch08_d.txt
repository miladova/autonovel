The fleet comes up with the boar, as it was always going to. All evening the grain ships of Alexandria have been sailing in the room's ballast — tonnage under the talk of fish, winter weather under the music — and with the third course Serapion brings them formally to anchor.

“The prefect of Egypt will want the sailings early this year,” he says, in the tone of a man discussing the weather at sea, which in his family *is* the weather at sea. “There is talk in the Canopic quarter of stores for an eastern movement. Grain follows talk the way gulls follow offal. It does not ask the talk's credentials.”

“Eastern movement,” Avidius says, tasting the phrase the way he tasted Ammonius' rebuilt sauce: carefully, and with the intention of having it again at his own prices. “The emperor's east is a rumour with a staff.”

“Most staffs begin as rumours. It is how they avoid the winter sailings.” Heron, who has the figures behind his eyes, lays them out on the table between the cups without paper: hulls available, hulls promised, the harbour dues at Ostia sitting in the middle of the numbers like a reef everyone at the table has hit in a different season. Antonia, on her circuit, hears the reef get its name — the same dues her landlord's agent, her husband-that-never-was, her whole triangle of paper and committee, have lately been navigating — and hears Avidius say, with the mildness of a man reading figures, which are his poetry, “A senator of my acquaintance will speak to the dues this year. Family reasons.”

The room does not pause. Rooms in her house are trained. But Diomedes, at the serving door, makes a note in the darkness behind his eyes to match Heron's, and Antonia files Avidius' sentence where she files all sentences that arrive with their price already worked out: between the quarters. A wedding for a harbour due. A committee concluded for a wedding. Paper in several directions at once. She has seldom been prouder of the seating: Serapion on one flank, the tax farmer watching his enemy civilised, the senator's nephew breathing hexameters by the musicians, and in the middle distance her own hostess's body, circulating like good coin, while the empire's breakfast next winter is arranged over boar.

“Your boar is from Hispania,” Serapion says to her, as she passes. It is not a question. In Alexandria men identify pork the way Romans identify accents: as intelligence.

“From a man outside Tarraco who owes the Greek at the Basilica an apology and expresses it quarterly in meat. Quality is the only apology that keeps giving evidence.”

Avidius laughs before he can file it. “She steals, Serapion. In front of witnesses. It is why the house is full.”

“I recycle,” Antonia says. “In this city everything has been somewhere else first. That is the dedication we're all performing.”

She says it lightly, for the table, and moves on; but Serapion's eyes follow her the length of the room with the expression of a customs officer who has just watched cargo volunteer its own manifest. Later — much later, in the baths version, where the evening will be misfiled under a death like a letter under a stone — men will remember the food and the singing and the terrible minute in the interval. Nobody will remember that the winter fleet was priced over the boar, and the harbour dues traded for a wedding, and a widow-maker of a story loaded by a bearded man from the Canopic quarter, all in the same twenty minutes, in a room hung with two hundred red flowers sewn by a girl across the river whose name the register will now never spell. That is not because the minute swallowed the evening. It is because the evening, like all Antonia's profitable ones, was a lattice of small, unglamorous decisions, and Rome has never known how to invoice a lattice. It can only carve the part that falls down.
