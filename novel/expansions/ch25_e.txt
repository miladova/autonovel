The house has acquired other, smaller revolutions under cover of becoming respectable. Daphne is teaching two mornings a week now: a banker’s niece with a stepmother problem and the daughter of a freed silk merchant whose Latin is excellent and whose respectability can be purchased by the half-hour. Her fees are paid into the strongbox in amounts she reports to Eirene as if filing dispatches from a successfully defended border.

“I am becoming capital,” she says.

“You are becoming less available for tragedy,” Eirene says. “Capital is a side effect.”

Philomela, whose flourish on her renewed contract would have caused a praetor to use the word *distinguishing* beyond his usual workload, has begun arranging harmonies for a group of women who sing at private funerals. “Grief pays promptly,” she explains. “And nobody asks for an encore in the atrium.”

A new musician arrives in spring with references, talent and an immediate instinct for the house’s filing of men according to how they behave around closed doors. She is twenty-six, from Tarentum, and wants her savings held where a husband cannot give them a tour of his debts. Antonia lays out the contract. The clause is cited. The musician reads it twice and asks the question that has become, in the Caelian admissions office, more reliable than an audition: “Who besides me has a copy?”

“The Greek. The registry. Diomedes in triplicate, which is a small country.”

“And if I leave in weather?”

“Settle promised work where people have paid for it. Take what is yours.”

“Do many leave?”

“Not since the front door started appearing in the documents.”

She signs, then plays something so cleanly that Ammonius comes out of the kitchen against all pastry discipline and stands with flour on one forearm like a sailor sighting home. “Hire her before the sauce notices,” he says.

She is hired. Her first public appearance is at a dinner where an aedile’s inspector is present as a private brother-in-law and tries, from force of administrative habit, to look official during the fish. Barbatus serves him wine with enough courtesy to disable a border post.

Later, in the accounts, Diomedes enters the evening under Ordinary Extraordinary. Antonia questions the category.

“It is where the house keeps events,” he says, “that are no longer news and not yet routine.”

“Peace, in working dress.”

“Peace does not itemise, madam. We remain its Secretary.”
