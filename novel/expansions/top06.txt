A dinner at which two houses are considering becoming a tide requires more staff than a small war and produces better intelligence. In the kitchen, quantities are being treated as diplomacy: too much luxury and the Avidii will suspect the Cornelii of covering debt with saffron; too little and the Cornelii will have filed themselves under decline. Servilia descends personally to inspect the fish because a woman who has run a household for decades learns that some metaphors must be gutted before guests arrive.

“Which course is the shipping interest meant to admire?” the cook asks.

“The one that arrives warm. Let admiration follow competence for a change.”

Marcus is intercepted on his way past the pantry by Onesimus, who removes from his hand a cup he has already filled with the optimism of a young man expecting to be bored. “Young master,” Onesimus says, “dinner is a succession of small doors. Enter later ones sober enough to know which house you are in.”

“I am always in my father’s house.”

“That,” Onesimus says, “is the ambiguity under management.”

Cornelia, in the music room, is trying out the Greek song at a pace her master describes as “maritime,” meaning apparently that it no longer hugs the coast of his instruction. He has been paid a fee Servilia described in the accounts as *continuing*, a word that has soothed his tendency to correct from the wrist. When he ventures that a bride should not sound “so knowing”, Cornelia asks whether knowing can be detected from the dining couches or only by professional appointment.

Her mother, entering at exactly the moment an answer might become useful to someone other than Servilia, says, “A bride should sound paid for. Sing the Roman one first.”

“And the Greek?”

“If the harbour asks.”

“It will ask in figures.”

“Then consider the figures foreigners.”

Downstairs Avidius the elder is being shown the dining room by Lentulus and appears to be pricing the past by the square foot. He has the expression of a man trying to work out whether three centuries are load-bearing. “A fine room,” he says.

“It has held worse decisions.”

“Most of mine took place near salt water. The room helped dissolve the evidence.”

It is not yet alliance. It is the sound of two kinds of Roman testing whether the floor complains under both.
