The language of a report is a public room with all its furniture turned to the wall. Postumus prefers it that way. He reads the draft aloud in Lentulus’s study, giving every cautious phrase the dignity of a man reading his own future inscription and finding the lettering tasteful.

“‘Prejudicial to established custom,’” he says. “That is good.”

“It is not good,” Lentulus says. “It is roomy.”

“In reports, roominess is a virtue.”

“In houses, it is called a corridor.”

A Secretary at the side table is making three copies because the original may have to survive being understood by men with appointments. Diomedes is not present—Diomedes is never present in rooms where the Senate writes about people who pay his house’s fees—but his habits have colonised Lentulus’s vocabulary to an extent the senator is only beginning to invoice. Every clause now asks, at the back of Lentulus’s mind, where the copy is kept and whose hand is allowed to be honest.

Postumus objects to a sentence about “conditions of service” on the grounds that it implies conditions might differ.

“They do differ,” Lentulus says.

“Not in the report’s preferred universe.”

“Then let the report apply for citizenship in ours.”

The Secretary pauses with his pen in the air, a young man learning that history sometimes turns up as an argument over adjectives before lunch. Postumus sighs with the profundity of a Roman whose principles have encountered paperwork. “You have become troublesome in a minor key.”

“I am told it improves audibility.”

“By whom, the water board?”

“By women with copies.”

That, finally, is too much even for a room arranged to prevent meaning. Postumus looks up. Lentulus looks level. The sentence has entered the study in the wrong hand, so to speak, and can no longer be removed without smudging. The Secretary does not write it. It goes instead into the air of the committee, where many true sentences spend a season before anyone invents a heading that can survive them.

When the draft is done, Lentulus keeps no special copy. That is new. The Greek will have one eventually; the house on the Caelian will have one sooner; the Senate will deny needing one. Rome, as always, is being governed by distribution.
