He walks home instead of taking the chair, which his household will hear about as a philosophy. The streets between the Basilica and the Palatine are full of the dedication's second day: workmen dismantling barriers, priests' boys reliving processions with sticks, a stallholder repainting his sign to include the word *imperial* in a hand that gets less certain toward the expensive letters. A client of his father's time, now very old and carried by a grandson who is visibly charging the whole transaction to his inheritance, recognises Lentulus and raises a hand like a standard being unfurled for the last time. Lentulus stops. This is also the work. Names are exchanged; a grandson is blessed in the formula; an old man is assured, in public, at a volume that recruits witnesses, that the Cornelii remember.

It costs four minutes and the price of a small farm in the currency his wife manages. Walking on, Lentulus does the sum he has been refusing since the portico: the committee's true arithmetic. For the registry to be policy, it must cost his friends something and be seen to. For it to be revenge, it must cost one woman everything and be deniable. A measure cannot be both at the full rate; Rome, which runs two sets of books as a civilisation — Greek and Latin libraries facing each other across the emperor's courtyard, each convinced it is the original — will ask him, in the baths, in the toilets, in the terrible clarity of other men's jokes, which book he is keeping. He has been telling himself the answer in committee language for a month. The language is holding together like a speech, and a speech, as his grandfather used to say while the masks were still being made of men he had known personally, is just a wall that has decided to be admired instead of climbed.

At the house the door slave takes his cloak and the day off him in the same motion. Servilia, from the garden room, calls — without raising her voice; the house is built so that she never has to — that the Acilii have sent fish, that his son is out, that his daughter has sung the Greek one, once, to the music master only, a distinction the girl has negotiated like a harbour due. Lentulus stands a moment in his own hall being reported to like a province. Everything is in order. That is the intelligence his committee has produced, on every front, all week: order, itemised, at other people's addresses.
