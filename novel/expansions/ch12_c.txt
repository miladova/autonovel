“By what route?” he asks at last. “Forgive the Basilica manners. In this building one asks for the route before the substance. It may be a—” His mouth finds a word he does not use. “A family technology.”

“Thread,” Antonia says. “A Greek buying it at the seller's price. A tanner's widow. A guild book with a better column than the Senate's.” She gives him the order of the evening in her own case, since he gave her the phrase, unpaid, and a debt is a debt in this room if anywhere on earth. The vats, Diomedes' patience, the hennaed fact, the money stopping when the men who paid to forget had finished dying. She does not give him Baiae. Baiae is not on this agenda; Baiae has been itemised once today already, by a dead man on wax, and she will not have her stairs performing in a banker's window room as evidence. She gives him the years as figures, because figures, she has it on good authority, are his poetry. “Nineteen. Arrived, as the guild so beautifully files it. Taught to read by a woman who counted stairs so they wouldn't count her. Counting, always, before you ask. It is apparently the family trade, on every bank of the river.”

“Alive and —” Lentulus stops. There are words a man's furniture contains that he is only now dusting. “Flourishing is a banker's word. Let me do better. Is she *unharmed*, Antonia, by the manner of her keeping?”

“She is harmed by everything Rome charges her class for, and by nothing her parents —” Antonia deploys the word the dealer's books never contained, “— in Transtiberim could keep off her with the money stopped. She is betrothed to the bakery. Her future mother-in-law inspects her reading as a risk to the yeast. Her father is dying at the pace water finds things, joking from the rail. She has been told nothing except by omission, which is this city's first language, as you and I have grammars to prove. So: no, she is not *unharmed*, Lentulus. Nobody in this story is, except possibly the crocodile in Serapion's version. She is alive, and pointed, and hers. That is the sum I found it under.”

At the window, not seeing the Forum's immortal afternoon in any sense a foreman would accept, Lentulus says, “I asked my father once.”

“I know the sentence he sold you.” She does not make him produce it. It is the one mercy available in the room at these rates. “Your tablet evidently invoices it better than I can.”

“It says the person wronged is not me.”

“Then for once the estate papers and I are in agreement. Do not make me say so in the Senate.” It comes out drier than she priced it, and he — damn him, damn the grey at his temples and the shoulder that aches in warm water and the boy of twenty-four still standing in the ruin like a column drum left on rollers — he comes within an ace of smiling, and the room does not change, and the drums below go on inching a war toward legibility, and that, she thinks, is how the world actually performs its earthquakes: in a banker's back room, between the courses, with nobody's hands doing anything recordable at all.

“My wife will have known the route too,” he says quietly. “Servilia hears things at the speed other people decide. When I tell her — when I confirm to her, after nineteen years of her knowing at oyster-depth — she will ask what I intend in the exact language of logistics. She made a marriage work like rain on a roof. It is only fair to warn a creditor.”

“Warn her that I count faster.” Antonia lets the lamp into it — the smallest warmth, as before, and then the lamp is trimmed and they are back to business, because business is what will fill a bakehouse share and keep a fuller’s guild book dull, and dull, in both their houses tonight, is a form of love Rome will never carve. “The settlement, Lentulus. Not charity, we are agreed. Not a scene, we are agreed twice, in your father's Latin and mine. Tools, extended. The oven share, through the guild as a Baiae bequest: the mistress her mother served, dying conveniently rich and childless, two quarters ago; Diomedes will draft her posthumously, he has a hand for the dead. A sum for Vibius's pyre that arrives as guild money, because a fuller who held a rail and joked is not to be buried at a senator's discount. Nothing with your name, my name, a date in the old emperor's winter, or a smell of the Palatine. If you cannot settle it without spending her, then, in the phrase your family is buying from me this year at a premium — *you are not to settle it*.”

“I can settle it.” He turns from the window decisively, the statesmanship reassembling over the forwarding address like a toga over a shoulder that aches. “Through Soter, through the guild, through the Greek's other rooms. Papers that point at Campania and die there. Servilia will see the drafts. She has a better eye for a hem—” He catches the metaphor arriving from across the river and lets it stand. “—for respectability, than any clerk alive. On those terms, Antonia.”

“On those terms,” she says, “the house accepts your father's apology, at the rate of interest he so carefully specified: none to himself, all of it forward.”
