The first version arrives before noon on a human back, as most of the house's essentials do. It is carried by a wine carter who once tried to smuggle Campanian past Barbatus in the dark and now overpays his debt to the house in news, delivered at the yard door with the amphorae: *the committee's done; it's general; you're all to be registered and nobody's to be closed before you've been apologised to by appointment.* He is tipped in soup. In the door-book, Diomedes enters him under *intelligence, vintage of*, with a note to adjust his cartage at the next quarter in recognition of services to the cellar's morale.

The second version arrives with the afternoon's first caller, a grain man from Puteoli who cancelled three bookings in the inspection weather and now wishes, with a merchant's memory for tides, to uncancel all three and add a fourth as ballast. “A house that's survived the Senate,” he says, settling into Antonia's presence like cargo into a hold, “is a house a man can do business in without his wife's brother writing letters. No offence to the committee.”

“None taken on its behalf. I file offences quarterly, and the committee has had its quarter.” She moves him from apology to menu in two sentences, because apology unconverted is a cost a house carries twice. Behind her, Diomedes enters the rebookings in the second hand, the one for facts that must survive fashion, and the house feels, along its corridors, the small arterial change of a calendar filling up.

The third version — the facts with their shoes on — Diomedes fetches himself from the posting place, having sent nobody who might improve it in transit. He has the text copied entire. This costs money. Money, in this house, is spent on paper the way other houses spend it on priests: early, regularly, and in the belief that disasters prefer unregistered premises. He lays the copy out on the kitchen's least floury table, because Antonia, who is tasting sauces against the return of the Alexandrians, conducting the foreign policy of the evening from beside a stove, will not leave the kitchen in the middle of a sauce for anything less than an emperor, a fire, or a clause.

“The registry is as madam was advised,” he says, reading as a man reads a bill he has already paid. “General. Quarterly. By appointment. Inspections at stated hours, by officials named in advance, in the public rooms.” He says *public rooms* in a way that costs Ammonius, at the great stove, an audible snort into the saffron. “Penalties for unregistered persons are struck out. In their place, a provision concerning house-keepers.” The stylus behind his ear stays where it is. In eleven years it has only once been taken down for emotion, and the kitchen was informed of it for a week. “Shall I read the provision, madam?”

“Read the provision, Diomedes.”

He reads it in his death-notice neutrality, standing in a kitchen in a house built out of stairs, and the women shelling peas for the Alexandrians stop shelling at *refusal* the way a room once stopped laughing between courses. Philomela, who is resting her voice upside down on a stool with her hair in a towel — a professional posture nobody feels entitled to question — sits up slowly, like an audience in one body. Daphne, who has been mending at the table's far end because in this house even the singers mend, puts the needle down carefully, so that history shall not later claim she dropped it.

“Again,” Antonia says, in the voice she uses for menus and mercy.

He reads it again.

Outside the kitchen window the courtyard performs its winter afternoon: the damp map of a country drying off the wall at last, Barbatus supervising a delivery of lamp oil with thumbs the size of small hammers, a girl with a basket walking the long way past the music room. Inside, nobody does anything with the sentence for a moment except keep it. Keeping, in this house's book and a fuller's across the river, is a complete action. Then Daphne says, “That is the sentence you gave him,” not as a question, because Daphne has the singer's disease and believes every room has a song under it, and today the song and the sentence have arrived in the same post.

“I gave nobody anything.” Antonia takes the tasting spoon back from wherever her hand has parked it in the air and returns it to the sauce, which has been waiting with a patience the Senate might study. “I described, in a banker's office, what a registry would have to contain if it was to be a registry and not a raid. A senator drafted. Men who draft should be encouraged with soup, Daphne, not attribution. Attribution is a tax.”

Philomela, upright now, with the towel slipping, says, “In Corinth we had a saying about generals who win battles other people planned: they get the processions, we get—”

“—the contracts,” Antonia says. “Yes. At the new year. Every woman in this house, with that provision cited in her own hand at the foot of her terms. Diomedes will prepare the copies. Yours, Philomela, may be in Greek, for people who'd know. Daphne's will be in a hand large enough for auditors.” She says it as housekeeping. The kitchen hears it as something else and files it as housekeeping anyway — that is how revolutions are entered in the first book, upright and dull, so that they may survive to be interesting in the second.
