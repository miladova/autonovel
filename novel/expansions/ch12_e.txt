Diomedes does not ask until the Caelian street. Even then he asks in bookkeeper's Greek, two paces back, at the gradient where the muleteers swear and a man's breath is his own again: “The account, madam?”

“Closed in the first book. Open in a fourth.” Antonia passes the fuller’s workshop whose lamps her house keeps filled at a neighbour's rate, and the dealer's daughter who was married out of her dining room waves from a door with a baby already itemised on her hip like good news. “We have acquired, Diomedes, a general registry, a clause in draft, an oven in Transtiberim and an understanding with a dead man's apologies. Enter the first three at their market prices.”

“And the fourth?”

“Leave the column blank.” Barbatus is opening her own door with the weather of him, lamps coming up by sections behind his shoulders, the house performing its evening overture: water, linen, Ammonius disputing saffron with a continent. “A completed book, somebody across the river says, is a dead one. We are in the keeping business, Diomedes. We leave spaces where the count isn't finished.”
