The creditor's name, for the record his father will never be offered in the study, is Lucius Hostilius, he is twenty-three, and he owes Marcus money at dice and pays in information because it is cheaper and, in the baths' economy, less taxable. He has the information today in the showy condition of a man who has carried it across three rooms without spilling the best part: still foaming. He delivers it at the exact moment the ball game breaks up, when young men are towelling themselves into temporary philosophers and a story can be tested against a whole audience of wet elbows.

“You'll like this,” Hostilius says, meaning the opposite, as men do when a story has a door in it other people may presently be invited to walk through. “The fullers' guild registered a dowry last week that their clerk — cousin of mine, a throat like a wine shop — says smells of the Quirinal through cedar. Baiae bequest, the papers say. Dead lady of property, remembering a laundress. Money for tools and a share in a bakery oven.” He could stop there. The baths, which have produced three false deaths in a fortnight and buried them at no expense, lean in as one man for the part they have really booked. “Only the bequest's papers were drafted in the Greek's outer office at the Basilica. The one whose small room —” He leaves the small room in the air between the warm room and the cold plunge like a song at the wrong speed, and young Avidius, who has been oiling himself with the industry of a man ballasting for weather, says without looking up, “Lucius. Plunge. Now. It's a rule with information.”

It is the first true act of friendship Marcus has been offered in a public place, and it arrives in the form of a shipping heir ordering a third party into cold water.

In the plunge Hostilius, penitent and blueing, gives him the rest of it the way men give evidence when the room has shrunk to a temperature: a needlewoman's name, a trade, a phrase in drink — *old money, Quirinal-handled* — a girl across the river who has been noticed, this winter, correcting senators' sons in dining rooms and being paid at a great house by the piece with the thread itemised separately. Avidius, up to his waist in the same water, helps the catching of it not at all, which is his own contribution: he becomes, for four minutes, furniture with a pulse. He does not say *I was in that dining room*. He does not say *my father's figures are about to be weather in your house*. He says, when Hostilius has been in long enough for the truths to cool to their right temperature: “You owe him the dice still. This is not a currency either of us banks.”

Marcus, whose education this winter has included, term by unpaid term, a returned necklace, a corrected sentence, a funeral at an edge, and the entire sound the Senate makes not answering, hears the arithmetic go up his mind the way it must once have gone up someone else's in a house with damp in one wall, counting with her mouth open on the number. *Nineteen.* A face at a doorway, with music. Dark brows. A girl who said *she isn't here to tell it wrong herself* about his sister — *his sister*, in every construction his tutors ever parsed, arriving now in duplicate like a verb with two endings and a will of its own.

The cold does the rest of his thinking for him, the way it did once for Acilius' counsel in another plunge on another floor of the family's plumbing. It clarifies. It makes the body's opinion of shouting unanimous. He climbs out, accepts the towel a slave hands him — Onesimus is not here today; this is the son's establishment, manned by a Felix of the rising generation who forgets at slightly cheaper rates — and says, to the room at large, in a voice he has never before used in public for the reason that until today he had nothing to spend it on: “A bequest from Baiae. Half the laundresses in Rome had mistresses there. The stairs alone would keep a guild of widows in property.” The laugh goes up where he has put it — at the stairs, never mind whose, at Baiae in the abstract, at the idea of laundry bequests as a class — and the story, deprived at its source of the only reaction that could have made it a fortune, subsides back into the baths' general weather: one more rumour in the steam, unminuted, walking home underneath its author's ribs at a pace very close to a religious procession already, except that no god Marcus has been brought up with uses doors in Transtiberim.
