When he has gone — rod, clerk, and a report already composing itself in his head in the passive voice, that great laundry of Roman administration, in which things *are found* and *are noted* and nobody in particular has ever found or noted them — the house exhales in a way it would deny doing. Ammonius sends up, unasked, a plate of the almond cakes that are reserved for officials and emergencies. Eirene takes one and eats it standing, which in Eirene is a parade.

“Well,” she says. “We are a den with a pastry cook. It is entered in the record.”

Diomedes is already writing the visit into the second book, in the hand he keeps for facts that must survive fashion. “He asked about Daphne's manumission twice,” he says, without looking up. “Once for the form. Once —” the stylus pauses, a toll-gate “— for the person who will read the form and ask him why he asked once. Somebody gave him her name before he came, madam. Not the register. A name, carried separately, like a stone in a sling.”

Antonia is still for a moment in the small dining room, where the inspector's cup sits with a finger's width of watered wine unevaporated at the bottom. She picks it up herself; a slave appears out of the house's well-trained air and, reading her face, decides to be needed in the corridor instead. “Whose stone?”

“The aedile's office would say the committee's. The committee would say the clerk's error.” Diomedes blots a line with the edge of his thumb, an Alexandrian trick he has never explained and she has never asked him to, it being house policy to let competent men keep one mystery each. “I have noted the hour he asked, and the hour he asked again. If a name travels, madam, it leaves footprints in other people's paperwork. You taught me that. In Naples, I believe, over a landlord.”

“I teach a great deal over landlords. It is my university.” She sets the cup down in its ring — she notices herself doing it and thinks of another house, a shrine room, a wife's hands, and files the thought where she files all thoughts with that address: between quarters, unopened. “Daphne is to be told her name was carried. Not frightened. Told. In this house nobody is protected by being uninformed; I have seen the other system work and it ends in Baiae.”

Eirene, who has finished the cake, says, “She'll want to sing about it.”

“She can sing about it in Greek, for people who know.” Antonia moves to the window. The street below is doing its noon business — a water-seller, a pair of advocates sharing a joke at a client's expense, a child herding three geese with a seriousness the Senate might study — all of it entirely uninformed that it has been represented this morning, on paper, as a neighbourhood suffering from congregation and noise. “Congregation,” she says aloud. “They have inspected a dining house and discovered dining. Lentulus must be beside himself with the quality of his intelligence.”

“Careful,” Eirene says, mildly. “That's almost a compliment to his organisation.”

“It is a compliment to his father.” The street, the geese, the advocates. “The old man could settle a province with a steward and a sealed tablet. The son sends a man with a rod to find a kitchen and is surprised by soup. Families decline, Eirene. It is the one inheritance tax nobody evades.”

Diomedes closes the book. “Shall I reply to the aediles' office today?”

“You shall reply on the third day, in the first hand, thanking them for their diligence and enclosing a copy of the tax receipts they already possess. Nothing discourages an office like being thanked with its own paperwork.” She turns from the window. The house is reassembling itself around her for the evening — she can hear the day shift handing over to the night one in a murmur of keys and menus — and her face, which has worn the inspection since the third hour like a good coat, puts it down somewhere the staff will not have to look at it. “And Diomedes. The next time a name arrives here carried separately, I want the name of the carrier. Buy it the way we buy thread.”

“By the piece,” Diomedes says, “and without informing their fathers,” and goes out to spend her money in the invisible denominations.
