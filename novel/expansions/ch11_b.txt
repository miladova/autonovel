The muster morning comes back the way these things do when a dead man starts initialling paragraphs: not as a scene but as smells in an order. Horse first. Then leather — the campaign chest open at dawn in a house that was putting a son into the army for a season the way it put a name into the Senate for a generation. Then his mother's women somewhere offstage, a corridor of voices in the part of the house he was already too old, at twenty-four, to be found in without an errand. He had an errand. He had, in fact, been manufacturing errands in that corridor for a year, with a rental roll as his passport and a copyist's quick hand for a view, and on that morning the corridor was empty of everything but light behaving as if nothing in Rome had changed addresses in the night.

He remembers asking the question only once and he remembers, with an accuracy his conscience has kept like a Greek keeps a strongbox, the use his father made of the pause before answering. The study smelled of wax. It is the detail that has over the years arranged all the others around itself: hot wax, seal-warm, in a room where land changed families and girls changed owners in the same hand, the hand now in front of him on the tablet, large, exact, gathering at the ends of lines.

“Was there a child?”

He had said it to the side of his father's face, a young man's profile held still by nothing but the house. His father had not looked up from the rolls. A province is being governed somewhere in a man's tone when he does that: the tone says the question has arrived without its paperwork and will be answered as a climate, not a claim. *There is nothing living with a claim on this house.*

Twenty-one years later, in the rain, with the tablet open under a lamp nobody comes to trim, Lentulus conducts the cross-examination he has been supplying internally, at his usual rates, since he was old enough to have rates. *Nothing living.* A phrase with room in it for a cradle across a river and a midwife's improved sum. *With a claim.* A phrase with a steward in it, settling things at a distance and charging the house for the mileage. *On this house.* As if the child might be living, might have a claim, but the house — the house was a fortress that language could be stationed around like a garrison. His father had not lied. Lying is for men who intend to be found out cheaply. His father had done the thing this family does instead, in excellent Latin, at length: he had chosen the currency, and he had made his son rich in it, and the son had spent it for twenty-one years, in committees, in a marriage, in the small hours, at compound interest.

You tell yourself, he thinks — second person, the pronoun men in his family keep for the minutes of the meetings they hold with themselves — you tell yourself you were twenty-four. Marcus is twenty. You have watched Marcus, this winter, be twenty in baths and dining rooms with a singer's glass necklace and a sister's face at a dinner table, and you have seen the exact moment, in a study with a duplicated grandfather, when the boy began paying for his arithmetic at something nearer the full rate. You were given the same moment, by a father with a seal in his hand, and you took the sentence as an answer because the alternative would have cost you your inheritance of yourself. It is very important, in what remains of the night, not to call that anything else. The tablet in front of you has not called it anything else. That is the courtesy the dead can still perform, when they have stopped needing the living to behave: they stop drafting.

*Brine preserves meat.* He reads the sentence again. His father, at the end, had a style his son had never heard at table: a kitchen style, unbuttoned, smelling of stores. Perhaps dying men write like freedwomen keep books — in the hand for facts that must survive fashion. Perhaps old Gnaeus, alone with his seal after a day of drainage, had at last met the one client the atrium had never admitted: his own morning, at twenty-four, on the other side of the desk, asking once. And had answered it, a generation too late, addressed to the wrong person. The tablet says so. The tablet — he has not read the rest; the rain is doing his childhood lessons in the basin, and the letters are gathering themselves like men deciding not to run — the tablet has the decency to know its own address is wrong.

The girl was not asked after. That is the sentence under all the sentences, the one the family did not inscribe. Antonia — the name she credits to herself; his father has that right too, damn the old man, damn his accuracy, arriving now like a bailiff with the right papers — Antonia was sold, was told, stood up, climbed. Lentulus is a man who has governed his opinions in public with distinction for twenty years. He has nothing to set against *climbed* except a career and a committee and an unsigned note about the order of the evening, sent this month by a hand that taught a girl the letter her name begins with. The arithmetic does itself, in stripes, through the shutters of whatever he still calls his conscience: he is not the wronged party in this document. He is its forwarding address.
