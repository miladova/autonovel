She does not, that evening, make a speech. The house would have minuted it in three hands and ruined the fish. But in the passage between courtyard and dining rooms she passes the place where damp once came through in the shape of an unvisited country and remembers January’s arithmetic: inspection before dawn; Barbatus in the doorway; the Greek’s rooms arranged like truths; a girl with a border and a singer with a laugh; a man in a chair smelling of ashes; all the ordinary fear of a woman who had built one life at full price and did not intend to have it surveyed by amateurs.

The wall is dry. It will not be dry forever. That is not pessimism, it is building management. Roofs go. Drains silt. Young men discover new geometries and have to be fed through them. A house is not a monument delivered from time; it is a local arrangement with time, serviced by people who know which stair complains and what a name looks like when it is being kept by its owner.

Diomedes is at the office table, closing one book and not the others. “The inspector has sent next quarter’s date,” he says. “An appointment so firm it amounts to flirtation.”

“Give him the almond cakes.”

“Already minuted.”

“And no doorway measuring unless he requests it as entertainment.”

“The schedule has a column.”

Eirene, whose hair has taken another colour toward iron this year and whose authority has compensated by darkening, comes through with a tray that is absolutely not a mirror. “Are we looking at walls for our morale now?”

“I am inspecting an asset.”

“Inspect one that can be eaten. There is a lamb developing strategy.”

From the great room Daphne begins the loom song, not the whole thing—three bars, a cap placed on the evening, for people who know. Somebody laughs before the accounting verse because some guests have become regular enough to have anticipations. It is, Antonia thinks, the most harmless form of tradition: a joke arriving in a place that has learned to hold its silences correctly.

She goes in to dinner by the front of the room, not the trades side. A small geography. Hers.
