The last evening, before the courtyard minute, has its own small fullness of incidents requiring no committee. A Spanish olive merchant arrives with a cousin and a grievance against shipping. A senator’s widowed sister, new to eating in complex houses since the word *respectable* began doing her family’s thinking for it, listens to Daphne’s loom song and sends a message to the kitchen for Diomedes. She wants, not the recipe, but the name of a good banker who will explain a statement without patting it.

Diomedes gives her the Greek and warns that the room is smaller than expected.

“Most useful rooms are,” she says. It goes into the door-book as an unattributed advance in civilisation.

Ammonius launches a sauce that causes two guests to propose marriages to separate relatives of his and one to propose a trading partnership. He declines all offers on the grounds of artistic solvency. Eirene removes an arena-grade drunk from the doorway by addressing him as a former colleague of Barbatus, which is historically inaccurate and operationally perfect.

At the main table the conversation turns, inevitably, to the emperor in the east. The war is discussed in the new style: with maps, confidence and an occasional uncharacteristic pause when somebody says *supply*. A young officer home on leave says the men marching out of Antioch now ask after letters “in plain Latin” from their families because quick meanings travel better in heat. This reaches Antonia through two courses and a pantry door. She says nothing. The house has learned not to make itself the footnote to its own evening.

Barbatus, at the street, encounters a girl with a tray who is running plates between two houses because her employer’s dinner ambitions have exceeded his domestic furniture. She almost drops a polished platter at the sight of a chair procession. He steadies it—not the girl, the tray—and tells her to hold it by the weight, not the shine.

Eirene sees this from the doorway and says, later, in the kitchen where all of Rome is revised: “That is the whole curriculum.”

“Enter it under Educational,” says Diomedes.

“Under Housekeeping,” says Antonia. “We are respectable now. We can let the truth in by the trade entrance first.”
