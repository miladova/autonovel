There are three of them besides the clerk, and they have clearly drawn, among themselves, the morning's choreography: the senior man asks, the second man looks, the third man exists to be the person nothing is said in front of, which in Roman administration is a load-bearing function. They view the dining room. The room — washed twice, hung as it was, set for nobody — gives them back the reflection they have come with: a good room, expensive, unremarkable in the way of places that are professionally discreet about being remarkable. The second man runs a finger along a couch frame and looks at it, disappointed by the absence of metaphor. The senior man notes the hanging, the way the inspector did before him — officials in this house always pause at Prima's border, two hundred instances of the same flower insisting on their differences, as if the room kept a register of its own. He does not write it down. There is no column for it. It enters the record anyway, in the margin where Rome files the things that actually persuade people.

Philomela's statement takes eleven minutes. Antonia has counted them against a water-clock the house does not possess, by Diomedes' breathing, which in the presence of paperwork keeps better time. The singer stands — she declines the chair, correctly, as a freeborn woman giving evidence in her employer's house — and gives them the fish, the metre, the crocodile, the laugh. When the third man, whose function is silence, unexpectedly asks whether the deceased had ever visited her rooms after the music, Antonia watches her walk it back like a bucket up a stair.

“Officer, after the music that evening there was no afterwards in this house for Gaius Calpurnius. Before the music there was fish.” She folds her hands over the place where, last night, a dead man's weight registered. “I can go back to the fish again, if the record is hungry.”

The senior man closes that door himself, with the air of a person shutting a draught on a colleague's behalf. Daphne follows, three minutes, pert as a tax receipt: she was resting her voice in the small room, she heard the story through the door, she came out when the laugh changed sound — “a laugh finishing the man instead of the story, officer; it is a singer's distinction and I ask the record's pardon for its accuracy.” Barbatus follows, mountainous, and gives them the street: watch passed, guests departing in order, the widow's house reached by a route he names with a soldier's nostalgia for distances properly walked. He is asked whether anyone left *early*. “The man left at the hour he was carried, officer,” he says. “Before that, nobody left who hadn't paid to stay. It is a dining house.”

In the kitchen Ammonius has arranged, out of whatever instinct makes Alexandrians lay ambushes with hospitality, a complete reconstructive exhibit of the fish course: the sauce (rebuilt), the fish (today's cousin), the little salted lake fish in a dish like a formation of silver boats. He invites the prefect's men to inspect it as men inspect a treaty. The second man, whose function is looking, looks. The clerk, whose function is writing, writes *kitchen in order* with the hopeless fluency of a man who has written it before in this house in a previous inquiry and will keep the phrase in his hand like a lover's lock of hair.

“You serve dormice,” the senior man says to Antonia. It is in his commission somewhere, probably under headings.

“I serve what rich men will pay to feel wicked about at a price that includes the absolution. Dormice are a tax on guilt, officer. The meat is incidental.”

“The deceased ate—”

“One. At the fourth course, with honey, while telling Philomela that elegiacs were a disease of his class. Nikias' account has the count, and the count of his wine, which I caused to be watered at his end of the table by house policy, because his physician wrote to mine last year and physicians, unlike committees, share their minutes.” This is new information and she gives it the way she gives everything dangerous: as paperwork, over a counter, with the seal already on it. “Diomedes has the letter. It is uninteresting as prayer. That is how the house files its foresight.”

The letter comes. The senior man reads it standing in the kitchen doorway with cakes cooling beside him like small pieces of contrary evidence against the whole theory of poisoners running pastry sections. His face does the thing her rooms specialise in: it tells the truth under the arrangement, for exactly one second, before he files it under conduct unbecoming. What it says, for that second, is that he has been sent by a competent prefect to audit a competently run house for the benefit of a senatorial uncle's grief and a committee's convenience, and that the paperwork — physician retained, wine watered by instruction, accounts deposited before the cock was thinking about it — is going to make the correct thing also the easy thing, a conjunction Roman officialdom experiences so rarely that it annotates it in the margins.

“The ring,” he says, because the second man, looking as assigned, has been looking at the box in Diomedes' office where it sits among the forgotten. “Serapion's. For the singer. It is in the Alexandrian's statement.”

“It is in the box,” Antonia says, “where it will remain until your inquiry has concluded, after which she may file it with her Greek like any other audience. If it were the price of a statement, officer, it would be on her hand and in the baths by now. I run a house, not a mint.”

He looks at her then — not the almost-smile; something drier, the respect officials pay to systems that will outlast their masters' instructions. “Madam,” he says, “I shall write that the house cooperated.”

“Write that the house was boring,” Antonia says. “Cooperation is a word with gleams on it. Walk it back to the fish.”
