He gets up with the tablet still open, which in a house of sealed things is a kind of rowdiness, and walks the night rooms the way his wife does her round — no noise until he is in a room, then the room owing him an explanation. The slaves have banked the kitchen fire. The garden door has been witnessed and barred, with a marginal note, delivered to nobody in particular by the door slave as Lentulus passes, that the young master is in and the rain is considering a treaty. Cornelia's door is shut, with Tyche's sandals outside it in the position of a small, loyal frontier. From behind it comes nothing but the sound sleep makes in a house where a girl has been well fed, well taught, and — Servilia's system again, in every wall he passes — well defended, mostly against her own family's conversation. He thinks of a settlement with a heading for musicians. He thinks of ballast, a word a shipping heir used at his table, and of girls as ballast in every house that has ever sent its cargo out under old names: his daughter, dowered and scheduled and singing on ration; another daughter, unregistered in any settlement, arriving in a fuller's guild book in the column for households; his niece — no. Let the arithmetic be clean, for once, in the second column. His daughters. The word does what Avidius' figure did when Servilia read it: it stops pretending to be anything else.

He writes nothing that night. This will surprise everyone who knows him by his paperwork, which is everyone who knows him. There is a draft composed — a man composes; it is his family's substitute for every other form of action — in which money is settled through the Greek (of course through the Greek; the Greek is Rome's confessional, with better acoustics) on a fuller’s household across the river, in the form of a legacy from a Baiae mistress neither alive nor safely dead enough to be invoiced. There is a second draft, worse, in which he goes himself. He can see that second draft as its intended recipients would: a senator in a doorway in Transtiberim with the ruined poetry of apology all over his good cloak, a dying man holding a rail, a girl hearing her life read back to her in somebody else's Latin like a contract she is expected to witness. *Drama is a tax the rich impose on the poor to pay for their remorse.* His father, dead nine years, graded out of a tablet, gets the best line in his son's worst night. It would be funny if the family kept its humour in rooms this small.

There is also — least drafted, most minuted in the body, where the Moesia shoulder files its weather reports — the question of Antonia, which is not, tonight, the question of the girl at all. She knows. He is certain of it with the certainty men of his experience reserve for harbours and wives: she will have had the information before him, cheaper, in the invisible denominations, with the thread itemised separately. She has held his paper at the Greek's for two quarters and held her peace at a higher rate. She returned a dead man's December bill marked settled and sent flowers that cost less, so it should not look like arithmetic. She instructs her people to answer in the order of the evening. Every draft of his that does not go through her first is, he understands with the clarity the masks specialise in after midnight, another sealed tablet: a settlement made about Antonia Cassia, by a Cornelius, in her absence, in a house that has been doing exactly that for three hundred years and calling the house honest.

So the first payment, when he makes it — not tonight; tonight a man is being unsealed at approximately the speed of drainage — will be information, delivered at a banker's back office, in daylight, with chairs. It is the only currency he has never tried with her. At twenty-four he gave her letters, which were luggage. At forty-five his committee gave her an inspection at dawn, which was theft in ministry's clothing. The thing in between, the thing he accepted a statesman's sentence instead of, is apparently the thing that must now be carried across the city in his own hands and set down, without a steward, without mileage. He does not look forward to it with any part of himself that is still his father's son. The rest of him — the part that told the prefect's business to be general, eventual and dull; the part that stood in an atrium at clients' hour seeing his daughter insert an unlicensed turn into music older than the family — that part is quiet in the way of a room that has finished being arranged.

He goes back to the atrium. The lamp is low. The tablet has waited, open, like a client who no longer needs the morning's performance. He reads the end again. *Choose better.* Two words; his father's whole late style, the brine and the kitchen and the unbuttoned truth of it. Under the mask, the sticking cupboard gives the case back to the dark with its usual sound: a small, drowned click, like a count coming right at the second attempt in another house entirely.
