# The Divided Harvest

*Warsaw, 1958. A novella in ten chapters.*

Zofia Krawiec and Tomasz Wierzbicki grew up in the same village, loved each other
at seventeen and twenty, and were separated by the burning of Brzozówka in June 1944.
Fourteen years later she is acting director of the Office for Religious Affairs and
he is a priest of thirty-four whom the Church has put forward, first on the terna,
for a bishopric. The state requires an opinion. The Security Service requires a signature.

## Note

This is a work of fiction. The political history it moves through — the Polish
October of 1956 and the closing of the thaw in 1958, the Office for Religious
Affairs, the 1949 decree of the Holy Office on Communists and the sacraments,
the preparations for the Millennium of 1966 — is real. The people are invented.
