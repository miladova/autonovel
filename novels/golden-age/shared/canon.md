# Series Canon — Historical & World Hard Facts (156–171 CE)

## Imperial Timeline & Succession
- **Antoninus Pius** (born 19 September 86 CE) reigns from 10 July 138 CE until his death at **Lorium** (12 Roman miles west of Rome on the Via Aurelia) on **7 March 161 CE** (the Nones of March), aged seventy-four, after eating Alpine cheese, developing fever, ordering the golden Fortuna moved to Marcus's bedchamber, and giving the night-tribune the watchword *Aequanimitas*.
- **Domitia Lucilla Minor** (mother of Marcus Aurelius, owner of vast Tiber brickyards stamped *EX PRAEDIIS DOMITIAE LUCILLAE*) died shortly before October 156 CE (c. 155–156 CE), leaving her clay-and-tile estates in trust for Marcus's children.
- **Marcus Gavius Maximus**, Praetorian Prefect for twenty years, dies c. 156–157 CE; succeeded by **Gaius Tattius Maximus**, who dies c. 160 CE and is replaced by dual Praetorian Prefects **Titus Furius Victorinus** and **Sextus Cornelius Repentinus** (promoted through the influence of Antoninus's concubine **Galeria Lysistrate**).
- **Quintus Lollius Urbicus** serves as *Praefectus Urbi* (Urban Prefect) until his death c. 160 CE, succeeded by **Quintus Junius Rusticus** early in the dual reign.
- **7–8 March 161 CE:** **Marcus Aurelius** (Imperator Caesar Marcus Aurelius Antoninus Augustus) compels the Senate to elevate his adoptive brother **Lucius Ceionius Commodus** as full co-Emperor (**Imperator Caesar Lucius Aurelius Verus Augustus**), promising the Praetorians `20,000 HS` (`5,000 denarii`) per man.
- **Consuls by Year (for brick-stamps, contracts & court dates):**
  - `156 CE`: Marcus Ceionius Silvanus & Gaius Serius Augurinus
  - `157 CE`: Marcus Vettulenus Civica Barbarus & Marcus Metilius Aquillius Regulus
  - `158 CE`: Sextus Sulpicius Tertullus & Quintus Tineius Sacerdos Clemens
  - `159 CE`: Plautius Quintillus & Marcus Statius Priscus
  - `160 CE`: Appius Annius Atilius Bradua & Titus Clodius Vibius Varus
  - `161 CE`: Marcus Aurelius Caesar (III) & Lucius Aelius Aurelius Commodus (II) — *the two heirs themselves are ordinary consuls on 1 January 161!*
