# Series Character Registry — The Ash and the Porphyry (156–171 CE)

Strict recurring named-cast discipline (~18 core named figures across Book I; minor guards, clerks, fullers, and litigants stay anchored by concrete role and physical detail per `DESIGN.md` cast-budget guidance so every named person hits like iron).

## I. The Dual Protagonists

### 1. Lucius Cassius Corvus ("Corvus")
- **Born:** November 140 CE (15 at Oct 156 opening; 18 by early 159; 20 in March 161).
- **Status at opening:** Freeborn Roman citizen (*ingenuus*, Crustumina tribe), penniless orphan of a drunken Sabine land-surveyor (*agrimensor*) who died in a collapsed clay-pit owing `3,200 HS` on an extortionate *chirographum* bond.
- **Appearance & Return Tag:** Lean, black-haired, pale grey-green eyes set slightly too deep under straight brows; a pale crescent brand across the knuckle of his right thumb where a heated bronze brick-stamp struck him at twelve. Walks with a surveyor's unconscious habit of pacing off room widths in five-foot Roman *passus*.
- **Voice & Lens:** Notices sightlines, load-bearing walls, cracked lintels, water-pipe diameters, coin weights, syntax errors in a man's Latin, and exactly who in a room stands nearest the door. Under-articulates grief into arithmetic and dry Sabine irony.

### 2. Lucida (childhood private name with Corvus: *Vespera*)
- **Born:** Late October 140 CE (15 at Oct 156 opening; 18 by early 159; 20 in March 161).
- **Status at opening:** *Latina Iuniana* (Junian Latin)—daughter of **Lucida Tyria**, a deceased Syrian-Phoenician dyer freed informally *inter amicos* whose reversionary patronal right (*ius patronatus*) has passed via debt-foreclosure into the hands of equestrian syndicate boss **Publius Vedius Pollio**. Free on the street; slave on her deathbed unless she wins *ius Quiritium*.
- **Appearance & Return Tag:** Dark olive skin, heavy blue-black hair smelling faintly of cedar-oil or alum mordant even when rich, amber-brown eyes that appraise fabric thread-count and lying mouths at a glance; fingertips faintly stained plum-brown at the cuticles from murex-substitute and walnut-hull vats in Book I's early years; a small dark birthmark like an olive-pip beneath her left collarbone.
- **Voice & Lens:** Notices smells (rot under perfume, cheap tallow vs. Campanian rose-oil, pregnancy, fear-sweat), textiles, hidden bruises under stolae, kitchen alliances, grain dampness, and who actually holds the household keys. Speaks three registers effortlessly: dockside Greek-Aramaic cant, brisk Subura bakery Latin, and velvety, lethal Caelian deference.

## II. The Rival Patrons & Antagonists (Invented Within the Historical Gaps)

### 3. Publius Vedius Pollio ("Vedius")
- **Role:** Primary Book I antagonist; wealthy equestrian syndicate-broker (`~6,000,000 HS`), owner of Subura *insulae*, Trans-Tiberim fuller-works, Emporium warehouse subleases, and the legal reversion over Lucida's freedom and estate; financier and procurer of luxuries for young **Lucius Ceionius Commodus**'s sporting circle.
- **Return Tag:** Plump, immaculately depilated forearms, smells of crushed bitter almonds and warm snow-water; peels winter pears with a tiny silver folding-knife while ordering evictions.
- **Contradiction:** Weeps genuine tears over dead songbirds and Greek tragedies while burning inhabited Subura tenements in dry August wind to collect ground-redevelopment leases.

### 4. Quintus Servilius Priscus ("Priscus")
- **Role:** Austere, ambitious patrician Senator on the Caelian Hill (`~12,000,000 HS`), former legate in Hispania, ally of **Junius Rusticus** and **Marcus Aurelius Caesar**'s anti-corruption circle—who takes young Corvus in first as a mud-scraping surveyor's boy and court clerk, then sharpens him into a forensic weapon against Vedius and the Ceionian faction.
- **Return Tag:** Stoop-shouldered, iron-grey cropped beard, chews dry fennel seeds for sour stomach, wears an unbleached toga one finger shorter than fashion to advertise Cato-like virtue.
- **Contradiction:** Preaches Stoic incorruptibility and equality of human souls from Epictetus while quietly acquiring confiscated estates through straw-freedmen (*interpositae personae*) and treating Corvus as a clever hound who must never forget his collar.

### 5. Ceionia Fabia *(Historical Figure — Sister of Lucius Verus)*
- **Role:** Daughter of Hadrian's first heir Lucius Aelius Caesar; thirtyish, brilliant, once betrothed in childhood to Marcus Aurelius before Antoninus broke the match so Marcus could marry Faustina. Holds court on the Caelian Hill as the steel spine behind her pleasure-loving brother **Lucius Ceionius Commodus**; becomes Lucida's most dangerous high-born patroness and mirror.
- **Return Tag:** Pale red-gold hair pinned with a single green emerald *acus* (six-inch hairpin sharp enough to puncture a tongue); speaks in low, rapid patrician clipped vowels without turning her head.

### 6. Gaius Ummidius Scaeva ("Scaeva")
- **Role:** One-eared former Praetorian *beneficiarius* turned chief enforcer, rent-farmer (*insularius*), and arson-broker for Vedius Pollio across the Subura and Tiber brickyards. Killed Corvus's clay-hut settlement in Chapter 1; stalks both protagonists across all five years.
- **Return Tag:** Missing left ear-lobe bitten off in a Dacian tavern thirty years back; carries a vine-wood *vitis* cane leaded at the tip.

## III. The Supporting Web (Subura, Courts, Ovens & Palaces)

### 7. Marcus Cornelius Fronto *(Historical Figure)*
- **Role:** Aged sixty-plus, North African consular senator from Cirta and Rome's supreme master of Latin rhetoric; tutor and tender correspondent of both Marcus Caesar and Lucius Commodus. Crippled by gout in knuckles and knees, grieving five dead infant children, sharp-tongued, vain about archaisms from Plautus and Cato. Tests Corvus's raw rhetorical gift in his Esquiline gardens.
- **Return Tag:** Wrapped in Libyan wool shawls in July, fingers swollen like ginger-roots around his ivory stylus.

### 8. Quintus Junius Rusticus *(Historical Figure)*
- **Role:** Stoic philosopher-senator, descendant of Domitian's martyred Stoics, closest political and moral counselor to Marcus Aurelius Caesar (and future Urban Prefect in Book II). Distrusts rhetoric, ornament, and clever climbers like Corvus—even while needing Corvus's dirty courtroom teeth.
- **Return Tag:** Coarse dark cloak without clasp, ink-blackened thumb, looks past men's chins at their sandals.

### 9. Marcus Aurelius Caesar ("Marcus Caesar") *(Historical Figure)*
- **Role:** Thirty-five to forty in Book I; Caesar and workhorse of the Empire under old Antoninus Pius. Sleeps on a plank covered with skins, coughs blood into linen in winter, loves his swarming babies and his cousin-wife Faustina while suffocating inside imperial duty.

### 10. Lucius Ceionius Commodus (later Emperor Lucius Verus) *(Historical Figure)*
- **Role:** Twenty-five to thirty in Book I; radiant, generous, कर्ज-drowned (*aes alienum*), adored by charioteers of the Prasina (Greens) and gladiators, underestimated by Stoics and dangerously underestimated by Marcus himself.

### 11. Habinnas Barba ("Barba")
- **Role:** Sixty-year-old scarred Cappadocian former mill-slave turned freedman master-baker (*pistor magnarius*) in the Subura/Trans-Tiberim; bitter, half-blind from forty years of flour-lung and oven-glare, grieving a dead son. Becomes Lucida's reluctant partner in the `100-modii-per-day` grain-mill gamble for statutory Roman citizenship.
- **Return Tag:** White flour caked permanently into the deep pockmarks of his cheeks; taps every wheat sack with a split brass reed to test weevil-hollow weight.

### 12. Tullia Crispina ("Crispina")
- **Role:** Thirty-two-year-old wealthy equestrian widow (inherited Puteoli glassworks and Ostian bottomry loans), sharp-witted, childless by secret choice, unapologetically sensual; takes nineteen-year-old Corvus as junior advocate and bed-companion in Part III, teaching him how money behind the curtains actually moves—and driving a white-hot spike of jealousy into Lucida.
- **Return Tag:** Wears two heavy green Campanian glass bracelets that chime once when she lifts her wine-cup.

### 13. Phileros
- **Role:** Shrewd, acid-tongued Greek *tabularius* (chief archive-freedman) in Senator Priscus's house and former clerk at the Temple of Saturn; knows where every erased wax line in Rome is buried; harbors a secret partner in the Emporium timber trade.
- **Return Tag:** Carries three bone wax-scrapers hung on a leather cord under his tunic like surgeon's knives.

### 14.Berenice ("Beroe")
- **Role:** Syrian-Jewish cosmetic chemist, midwife, and abortionist above the Argiletum fulleries; Lucida's dead mother's old acquaintance who gives fifteen-year-old Corvus and Lucida their first six-foot garret floor-space—for quadruple rent and night-errands—and teaches Lucida how Caelian wives pay fifty denarii in silence for lead-white, crocodile-dung facial paste, and rue-and-pennyroyal draughts.
- **Return Tag:** Smells of bitter myrrh and vinegar; one milky cataract eye she never bothers to cover.

### 15. Aulus Volusenus Niger ("Volusenus")
- **Role:** Aristocratic twenty-year-old patrician pupil in advocacy, nephew to Priscus, silk-sandaled bully and rival junior pleader to Corvus in the Centumviral courts—until Vedius buys his gambling debts and turns him into Lucida's dangerous suitor/pawn.
- **Return Tag:** Habitually twists a carnelian signet ring carved with a racing four-horse *quadriga*.

### 16. Sura
- **Role:** Twelve-to-seventeen-year-old mute Gallic stable-and-oven girl rescued by Lucida and Corvus from Scaeva's burning mill-lockup; fierce, observant, communicates in sharp hand-signs and slate-chalk; becomes the living conscience between them—making the cost of their fracture in Part V unbearable.
- **Return Tag:** Burn-scarred left forearm wrapped in dyed madder linen; whistles two low thrush-notes through her teeth to warn of lictors or Scaeva's men.
