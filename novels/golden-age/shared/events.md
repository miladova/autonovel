# Inter-Book Event Ledger

## E-001: Flight from Figlinae Subortanae and the October Horse Riot
- date: 0156-10-15
- location: Figlinae Subortanae, Campus Martius, and the Subura Firewall, Rome
- present: [Corvus, Lucida, Scaeva]
- canonical: Scaeva burns the squatter clay-shacks north of the Milvian Bridge; fifteen-year-old Corvus and Lucida escape with the bronze brick-stamp and wax tallies, enter Rome during the Ides of October chariot sacrifice, and vanish through the Firewall arch into the Subura during the ward brawl for the horse's head.
- rendered_in:
    book-one/ch_01: Corvus's POV, from the river-fog kiln fight through the Campus Martius sacrifice and Firewall brawl.

## E-002: Death of Antoninus Pius and the Night of Two Emperors
- date: 0161-03-07
- location: Basilica Julia, Aerarium Saturni, Castra Praetoria, and the Firewall Arch, Rome
- present: [Corvus, Lucida, Ceionia Fabia, Lucius Verus, Marcus Aurelius]
- canonical: As Antoninus Pius dies at Lorium on the Nones of March 161 CE, Scaeva raids Barba's bakehouse thirty-four mornings short of Lucida's statutory freedom while Corvus ruins Vedius Pollio per aes et libram across Saturn's scales—crossing into the equestrian order—and Lucida delivers Priscus's Syrian letters to newly hailed co-Emperor Lucius Verus at the Castra Praetoria, winning full Roman citizenship by imperial rescript and severing her bond with Corvus at dawn beneath the Firewall.
- rendered_in:
    book-one/ch_29: Corvus's POV, executing the Aerarium Saturni receivership and finding Barba dying in the Lautumiae.
    book-one/ch_30: Lucida's POV, winning her rescript from Verus at Castra Praetoria, burning her dead, and confronting Corvus at dawn.
