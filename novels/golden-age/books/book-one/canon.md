# Book One Canon — By Bronze and Balance (156–161 CE)

## Protagonists & Opening Facts
- **Lucius Cassius Corvus:** Born November 141 CE; *ingenuus* of the Crustumina tribe; right thumb knuckle scarred in a pale crescent by a hot brick-stamp at age twelve; father **Gaius Cassius Corvus** (former surveyor/bailiff) died in a clay-pit collapse owing `3,200 HS` on a *chirographum* bond.
- **Lucida:** Born late September 141 CE (childhood name between them: *Vespera*); *Latina Iuniana*, daughter of informal freedwoman dyer **Lucida Tyria** (deceased); dark olive-pip birthmark below left collarbone; patronal reversion (*ius patronatus*) held by equestrian **Publius Vedius Pollio**.
- **Shared Token:** A cracked terracotta master brick-impression from *Figlinae Subortanae*, split cleanly in two halves in October 156 CE.
- **Entry to Rome:** 15 October 156 CE (Ides of October — festival of the *October Equus*) via the Porta Flaminia and Campus Martius into the Subura beneath the hundred-foot *lapis Gabinus* Firewall of the Forum of Augustus.
