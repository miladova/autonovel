# Book Two Seed — Two Suns in the West (161–166 CE)

Ages 20–25. Rome has two Emperors for the first time in history: Marcus Aurelius on the Palatine and Lucius Verus departing in golden indolence for the Parthian War. Corvus (now an equestrian *advocatus fisci* and water-and-works commissioner in Rusticus's faction) and Lucida (now *Lucida Augusta-liberta* by imperial rescript of Verus, controlling Ostian grain-lighters, Caelian tenements, and Ceionia Fabia's private credit chest) wage five years of merciless, intimate war across flooded Rome, Ostia's hexagonal harbor, and Baiae's sulfur villas.
