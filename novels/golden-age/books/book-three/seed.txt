# Book Three Seed — The Auction of Trajan (166–171 CE)

Ages 25–30. Lucius Verus's victorious Triumph in 166 CE brings gold, Parthian captives, and the black pustules of the Antonine Plague into Rome. Two thousand die a day; ox-carts pile corpses outside the Esquiline; old senatorial houses go extinct in a fortnight; Verus chokes in his carriage at Altinum (169 CE); and Marcus Aurelius holds his two-month auction of imperial treasures in the Forum of Trajan to fund the northern legions. Amid a dying Golden Age, Corvus and Lucida's decade-long blood feud turns inside out into a lethal alliance and an earned, unsparing love.
