# THE FREEDMAN'S CHILDREN — working outline (SPOILERS: author's file)

Dual close third, past tense. F = Fausta POV, C = Celer POV. ~6,000 words
per chapter, 2–3 scenes each. Register: Mantel — close, dry, physical,
the sentence that turns on itself, the body in the room, power as what
people do with their hands and their silences. "She, Fausta" / "He,
Celer" disambiguation where needed. No weather openings. No prologue.

Supporting cast (fixed):
- Hilara — F's mother. Lodging house "at the sign of the Dolphin".
- Hermes — C's father, Augustalis. Second wife Vibia Procula (freeborn,
  thin, pious, not a fool). Baby half-brother Hermes names Lucius.
- L. Laecanius Bassus — patron, ~60. Villa at Baiae. Son Laecanius Rufus,
  ~28, quaestorian, needs praetorship; will go to Dacia on Trajan's staff
  as tribune. Daughter Laecania Paulla, ~17, to be married.
- Nicias — Greek tutor, hired by Hermes, slave of Bassus on loan.
- Thallus — Bassus's steward at Baiae, freedman, dangerous, owed money by
  everyone, keeps the house's papers incl. Hilara's manumission draft.
- Antiope — girl who works upstairs at the Dolphin some seasons; F's
  older-sister figure.
- In Rome: Pompeia Celerina's house → no. Use invented: the house of
  **Cornelia Tertulla**, widow of a consular, Bassus's cousin, who takes
  Fausta as a "companion" (reader/attendant) — a Palatine-adjacent matron
  in Plotina's circle. Her steward **Phaedrus**. Her son **Cornelius
  Priscus**, 24, who rapes Fausta in bk1 ch 9 (this is the house that
  "breaks her once").
- **Marcia**, Plotina's freedwoman and ornatrix, who becomes F's teacher
  of the Palatine. **Plotina**, **Matidia**, young **Sabina** (child),
  **Hadrian** (28 in 104).
- In Dacia: **Rufus** (patron's son, tribune); **Sex. Attius Suburanus**?
  no — use invented centurion **Marcus Valerius Crescens**, primus pilus
  type who sees C's worth; **Sura** (real) whose staff C joins as a
  clerk; **Decebalus** seen at the surrender 102 and in the pursuit 106;
  **Tiberius Claudius Maximus** (real! the cavalryman who brought
  Decebalus's head) cameo; **Apollodorus** building the bridge.
- Secretariat: **Cn. Octavius Titinius Capito** (real, ab epistulis under
  Trajan, equestrian) — C's eventual chief. Freedman under-secretary
  **Abascantus** (invented, named after Domitian's) who is the last of
  the old kind and takes C as his hand.

Engine of the break:
- C's betrayal of omission (ch 20/27): when Hermes and Bassus arrange
  Fausta's placement/transfer and later when her mother's manumission is
  challenged by Thallus/Bassus's heirs, C has a letter he could send to
  Sura's office / Rufus to protect her and does not, because Hermes's plan
  (equestrian census for C via Rufus's gratitude) requires Bassus's
  goodwill. He tells himself she's safe. She isn't.
- F's act of commission (ch 24–26): in Cornelia Tertulla's house / then
  Marcia's service, to secure Hilara's manumission confirmation and her
  own footing, F gives Plotina's circle information about Bassus's
  finances and Hermes's shipping contracts (the grain fraud at Puteoli —
  Hermes has been short-weighting the annona). This lands on Hermes's
  house: the contract is revoked, Hermes is fined, dies of a stroke in
  107 (ch 28). C believes she did it from contempt. She didn't know Hermes
  would die; she did know the house would be hurt and chose her mother.

Chapter plan

1  F — Puteoli, Feb 98. Nerva's death reaches the port with the first
   ship; the Dolphin; Hilara; Antiope; Fausta sent with a message to
   Hermes's house; Celer; the tutor's room; the two children on the mole
   watching the Alexandrian fleet not arrive. Establish everything.
2  C — Hermes's house; the tomb inspection; Vibia Procula; Nicias;
   the Baiae villa summons: Bassus's salutatio for his freedmen; Thallus;
   Rufus home from Rome with news of Trajan; C told he will go to Rome
   with Rufus "when the time comes". C tells F on the shore; she already
   knew (Antiope heard from a client). First small lie.
3  F — Spring–summer 98. Baiae from the fishing boat: the great naked.
   Hilara's manumission: F learns from Thallus (who visits the Dolphin
   for his cut) that her mother's freedom rests on a codicil. Hilara
   and Thallus upstairs. F and C in the Serapeum; the Isis festival of
   the ship (Navigium Isidis, March) — the first explicit awareness of
   the body between them, unspoken.
4  C — Autumn 98–99. Trajan enters Rome (Oct 99) — C goes with Hermes and
   Rufus to see it; first sight of Rome, the Palatine, Plotina on foot.
   Pliny glimpsed. Hermes's grain contract negotiation with the
   praefectus annonae's office; C sees how it's done (and the
   short-weighting). Back in Puteoli C is forbidden the Dolphin: Hermes
   has heard. He goes anyway; F's room; Antiope; nothing happens; everything.
5  F — 100. Paulla's betrothal at the villa; freedmen's families attend
   as servitors. F waits on the women; Cornelia Tertulla visiting from
   Rome notices her reading. F overhears Hermes and Bassus settle C's
   future. Hilara tells F what Bassus did to her and when; F does the
   arithmetic about her own face. (Father = Bassus, strongly implied,
   never confirmed in bk1.)
6  C — 101. Trajan marches (March 101). Rufus appointed tribune; C goes
   as his secretary-client, age 14. Leaving: Hermes's instructions, the
   tomb, Vibia's blessing, the night before with F on the mole — the
   promise (letters). First explicit near-scene: they kiss; she stops it;
   he thinks it's refusal; it's terror of Hilara's life. Departure.
7  F — 101–102. The Dolphin without him. First letters (two, then none —
   they're being read by Thallus; she doesn't know). Antiope's abortion;
   F helps; Hilara's pragmatism. Cornelia Tertulla's letter: she will
   take the girl as companion. Bassus consents — it costs Hilara the
   codicil's security. F goes to Rome, 15.
8  C — Danube 101–102. Winter camp, Tapae, Adamklissi aftermath; Rufus
   is a mediocre tribune; C does the paperwork, logistics, learns Dacian
   from a prisoner; centurion Crescens; the surrender of Decebalus at
   Sarmizegetusa (102) — C writes the inventory of the terms. First
   killing (a prisoner, ordered). Sura notices the inventory.
9  F — Rome 102. Cornelia Tertulla's house on the Caelian. Rules.
   Phaedrus. Mistakes: F speaks Greek to a guest; sits; reads aloud too
   well. Priscus. The rape (explicit, from inside, unflinching, short).
   Cornelia knows and does nothing; teaches F the lesson of it as a
   kindness. F doesn't write to C about it. She writes about the house.
10 C — 102–103. Rome with Sura's staff; the triumph of 102; C sees the
   Palatine from inside as a clerk; sees F across a courtyard at
   Cornelia's (Sura dines there); their first meeting since — formal,
   wrong, a slave in the room. She is altered; he reads it as
   superiority. He is altered; she reads it as Rome. Letters stop.
11 F — 103. Cornelia takes F to the Palatine: Plotina's women's court.
   Marcia. The rules of the rooms. Matidia; little Sabina. F is useful:
   accounts, Greek, memory for faces. Priscus again — F now arranges to
   be elsewhere; the first manoeuvre. Cornelia's price: F reports on
   Bassus's letters.
12 C — 103–104. Secretariat: Titinius Capito's office; Abascantus.
   Hermes visits Rome: the plan — Rufus's gratitude → equestrian census
   for C by 25. Hermes mentions F "that girl's gone up in the world on
   her back" — C hits his father. Then apologises. Then does what he says.
   C's sexual life: a Palatine slave, Chrestê — explicit, transactional,
   he's kind and it's still ownership; he knows it.
13 F — 104. Hadrian at Plotina's court (28, Greek accent, mocked). F sees
   Plotina choose him in a look. Marcia teaches F the long game. Letter
   from Hilara: Thallus wants more; Bassus is ill. F asks Cornelia for
   leave; refused. She asks Marcia; Marcia says: ask for something you
   can give in return. First trade.
14 C — 105. Second Dacian War. C back north with Sura; the bridge
   (Apollodorus); Rufus's cowardice at the Danube crossing covered by C's
   paperwork. Crescens dies. C's second killing isn't ordered. Letter from
   Hermes: Bassus dying; Thallus holds the papers; "do nothing, the house
   is with us."
15 F — 105. Bassus dies. F learns at the Palatine before Hilara knows.
   Rufus inherits; Thallus becomes the heirs' man; Hilara's codicil
   is "under review". F goes to Puteoli with Cornelia's grudging leave
   (traded for Bassus's letters). The Dolphin; Hilara older; Antiope
   gone. Thallus's offer: F for the codicil. She refuses; he says, we'll see.
16 C — 106. Sarmizegetusa falls; the hunt; Decebalus's death (Claudius
   Maximus); C writes the inventory of a kingdom — the gold, the people.
   The document he's proudest of: the list that organises the sale of
   50,000 Dacians. Letter from F (first in two years) asking him to write
   to Rufus about Hilara. He drafts it. He doesn't send it: Hermes's
   letter says Rufus is "sensitive about the steward". Omission.
17 F — 106. No answer from C. F's decision: Plotina's circle wants to
   know about the annona at Puteoli (Sura is auditing); F knows Hermes's
   books because she read them at twelve. She tells Marcia what she knows
   is true. Marcia says: this will be used. F says: use it. (She thinks
   it will cost Hermes a fine and buy her mother's freedom. It does both.)
18 C — 106–107. Return to Rome. Promotion. Sees the audit file on
   Hermes's contracts cross his desk; recognises the detail; knows the
   source. Confronts no one. Goes to Puteoli: Hermes's stroke; the
   tomb; Vibia; the contract revoked; the house's debts. Hermes dies.
19 F — 107. Hilara's manumission confirmed by Rufus (Plotina's word via
   Cornelia). Hilara doesn't know what it cost; thinks Thallus relented.
   Thallus tells F what C didn't do. F burns C's letters. The Dolphin
   sold. Hilara to Rome as F's dependant.
20 C — 107. The funeral; the tomb filled; Thallus; C learns from Thallus
   what F did (Thallus plays both). C's grief turns to shape. He writes
   to F once: three lines. She doesn't answer. He takes Chrestê's freedom
   and sends her away; he will own no one. He will be owned by Rome instead.
21 F — Triumph, June 107. 123 days of games. Plotina's box. Sabina.
   Hadrian. F in the women's orbit now, seen. Her mother in the crowd.
22 C — Triumph. The secretariat's view; Capito; the lists of spoils
   (his). He sees her in the box. She sees him among the clerks.
23 F/C — the meeting at the triumph: across the Forum under construction.
   What each says. Too late / too early.
